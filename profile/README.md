We're a young team that moves fast and loves making things. Every product we ship has its own name, mark and colour, and all of them share one design system and one typeface, so each carries a trace of Sorena.

### What we make

Mobile apps, games and desktop apps. Watch and car apps come next.

### How we work

- **People lead.** Every idea, every design and every decision comes from our team.
- **Fast, without cutting corners.** We move quickly and finish every product by hand. AI is one of our tools: it speeds up the work, but the thinking and the taste are ours.
- **Craft in the details.** Every screen is checked on phone and desktop, in light and dark, with a screen reader. Touch targets are at least 44px and motion stays calm.
- **One system underneath.** Every product builds on our shared design system, so our time goes into what makes each one different.
